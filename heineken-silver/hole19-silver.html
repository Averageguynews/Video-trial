HOLE 19 · THE SILVER LINING

Heineken Silver low carb launch · mini golf bar activation concept · approx. 1.0 m × 3.0 m module

PAR 3

TOP-DOWN LAYOUT

WHAT'S ON THE HOLE

1

Red Star Tee

Charcoal tee mat with a silver dashed border and the red star as the tee spot.

2

Silver Fairway

Silver-grey turf mown in stripes, framed by brushed-aluminium rails. It reads as Heineken Silver from across the bar.

3

The Carb Trap

A narrow sand bunker on the left rail, plus a small pot bunker on the right. It's a low carb hole, so stay out of the carb trap. The centre line is open for a straight putt over the bridge.

4

The Ice Canal

Ice-blue water turf with floating ice floes, crossed by a narrow brushed-silver bridge. It plays on Heineken Silver's ice-cold theme.

5

The Silver Green

Heineken green putting surface with a silver Heineken Silver badge inlaid. The cup sits on the red star.

BAR RULES

  • Ball stops in the Carb Trap: +1 stroke, play it where it lies.
  • Ball stops in the Ice Canal: +1 stroke, replay from the bank.
  • Hole-in-one on the red star: ring the bell, win a prize.
  • Max 6 strokes, then grab your ball and head back to the bar.
