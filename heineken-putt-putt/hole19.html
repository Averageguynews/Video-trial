HOLE 19 · THE AMSTERDAM CANAL

Heineken-branded mini golf hole · bar activation concept · approx. 1.0 m × 3.0 m module

PAR 3

TOP-DOWN LAYOUT

WHAT'S ON THE HOLE

1

Red Star Tee

Black tee mat with the Heineken red star as the tee spot. The start point people will photograph.

2

Heineken Green Fairway

Signature green turf, mown in alternating stripes, with black rails and white pinstripe. Replaces standard green.

3

The Bunker

Narrow fake bunker in sand-coloured turf hugging the left rail, plus a small pot bunker on the right. The centre line is open, so a brave straight putt can run up and over the bridge.

4

The Canal

Blue water turf across the full width with wave graphics. The only dry way over is the narrow arched canal bridge.

5

Canal House Gate

Cut-out Amsterdam canal houses (a nod to Heineken's home city). Three doorways: two wide side doors, plus the narrow centre "Star Door" lined up with the cup.

6

The Star Green

Lighter turf green with a red star inlay. The cup sits in the centre of the star.

BAR RULES

  • Ball stops in the bunker: +1 stroke, play it where it lies.
  • Ball stops in the canal: +1 stroke, replay from the bank.
  • Through the Star Door and in: ring the bell, win a prize.
  • Max 6 strokes, then grab your ball and head back to the bar.
